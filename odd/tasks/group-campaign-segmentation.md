Selecting an establishment is a distinct direct mode. Show matched recipients/count and empty/error states, preserve create/edit validation input, reject empty resolved audiences.

Group business type is optional descriptive classification/selection aid. Campaign business type comes from each device's linked establishment, never inferred from group classification; devices without an establishment do not match a business-type or establishment filter. Optional group location reuses existing Google Maps Places component/configuration without resurrecting retired locations. Existing single-group membership is retained and moves are disclosed visibly before saving; assigning a group does not change establishment association or device settings. Preserve internal z2/wl35 IDs and visible42/35 labels.
